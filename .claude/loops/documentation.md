# Documentation Loop

## Common guardrails

- Follow `.claude/CLAUDE.md`, repository rules, and data-access restrictions.
- Do not commit, push, deploy, release, or promote artifacts without explicit approval.
- Prefer the smallest reversible change.
- Maintain `.claude/automation/runtime/current-task.md`.
- Stop at the iteration budget or any human approval gate.

1. Identify the authoritative source and intended reader.
2. Verify every command, path, option and claim against repository evidence.
3. Update the smallest authoritative document; avoid duplicating truth.
4. Preserve the Obsidian conventions in `.claude/CLAUDE.md`.
5. Validate links, examples and dates.
6. Finish through `/verification-gate`.
